What to include in your brief

  • Tell us how the product is displayed and whether it also needs to ship directly.
  • Share the number of items, their sizes and any required inserts.
  • Describe the opening experience, colours and finish you have in mind.

What to check before ordering

  • Compare folding cartons, corrugated presentation boxes and separate-lid options with the supplier.
  • Check whether the proposed sample demonstrates fit only or also final print and finish.
  • Confirm the production artwork template before arranging your design.

Quantity, timing and samples

Minimums and lead times depend on your specification and the supplier. Tell us your target quantity and delivery date; we will check availability before you commit.

Ask about a physical sample. Availability, cost and whether it represents fit, material or final print are confirmed for the proposed pack.

What affects your quote?

Size, material, construction, quantity, print coverage and finishes affect production cost. Tooling, setup, delivery and applicable taxes also need to be accounted for.
