PPWR supplier pack

A manufacturer file for the box: bill of materials, substances statement, empty-space note and an EPR map. You still draw up the Declaration of Conformity. We do not file your schemes.

Who and what

Not a Declaration of Conformity PPWR requires an EU DoC for packaging placed on the EU market. The brand (or the party whose name is on the pack) issues that document. This pack is the supplier story they attach: fibre, inks, coatings, substances, void. Unfold is not your authorised representative.
