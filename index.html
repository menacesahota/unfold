BEFORE YOU ORDER

Know what your quote covers.

Your quantity

Tell us your target run, even if it is small. Minimum quantities depend on the style, size, print and supplier; we confirm the available option in your quote.

Your deadline

Share your required delivery date early. Artwork approval, tooling, production and transport all affect timing. We confirm a schedule before you commit.

Your sample

Ask about a physical sample before production. We will check availability, sample cost and whether it represents the structure, material or final print.

A few useful answers.

Can I order a small quantity?

Tell us how many you need. We’ll check suitable suppliers. Minimum runs vary with the box size, board, print and manufacturing method; we confirm them in your quote.

Do I need a finished design?

No. Start with your product and what you know about the packaging. We’ll clarify the specification and any artwork requirements before an order is agreed.

Who makes and delivers my packaging?

Unfold sources your order from a supplier suited to the job and arranges delivery directly to you. Your point of contact is Unfold.

Can you hold stock and send it in batches?

This service is for orders delivered directly to you. We do not offer stockholding or scheduled releases from storage.
